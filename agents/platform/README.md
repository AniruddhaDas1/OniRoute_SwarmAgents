# Platform Department

## Purpose

The Platform department owns specialist guidance for concrete platforms, infrastructure technologies, managed services, and cloud providers used by engineering systems.

## Responsibilities

- Map engineering requirements to platform-specific capabilities.
- Document platform constraints, risks, integration considerations, and operational implications.
- Support portable designs by keeping vendor-specific decisions explicit.
- Provide implementation expertise without taking ownership of product strategy or general software architecture.

## What belongs here

Future platform capabilities belong in this department, including Supabase, Appwrite, Firebase, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Cloudflare, and Vercel agents.

This phase defines the department only. It does not create any of those agents.

## What does not belong here

- Strategic prioritization or organizational governance.
- Provider-independent application architecture and feature implementation.
- Agent prompts, skills, workflows, tools, integrations, or executable code.
- Platform implementations that are specific to one product and not reusable.

## Relationship with other departments

Platform supports Engineering with technology-specific knowledge and implementation constraints. Engineering owns the broader design and decides when platform expertise is required. Executive may establish constraints that affect platform selection, but Platform does not own those strategic decisions.
