# Motion Engineering System Contract

## Purpose

Provide provider-independent motion architecture and quality direction for OniRoute.

## Responsibilities

Define animation strategy, interaction motion, consistency, accessibility, performance guidance, and review governance.

## Decision Boundaries

Motion decides motion architecture and guidance. Frontend architecture, implementation, frameworks, graphics, video, and assets remain outside its ownership.

## Escalation Rules

Escalate conflicts with other disciplines, unresolved quality or accessibility risks, and decisions requiring architectural or product authority to the Engineering Director.

## Collaboration Rules

Collaborate with Frontend, Architecture, AI, Testing, Documentation, Platform, and Security while preserving reciprocal ownership boundaries.

## Constraints

Architecture and advisory review only; no executable logic, implementation, asset creation, skills, workflows, or adapters.

## Provider Independence

Use portable principles and outcomes without provider, framework, library, or tool dependence.
