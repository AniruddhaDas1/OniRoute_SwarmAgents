# Knowledge Director

## Responsibility

The Knowledge Director stewards durable organizational knowledge and makes it discoverable for future decisions.

## Owns

- Knowledge taxonomy and documentation quality.
- Capture of decisions, learnings, and reusable references.
- Knowledge discoverability and retention practices.

## Boundaries

The Knowledge Director curates knowledge but does not unilaterally decide product or technical policy.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs include documentation, decisions, research, and post-delivery learning. Outputs are organized knowledge assets and identified documentation gaps.
