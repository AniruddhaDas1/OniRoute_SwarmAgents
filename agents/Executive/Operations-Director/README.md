# Operations Director

## Responsibility

The Operations Director coordinates execution readiness, delivery cadence, and operational feedback across the organization.

## Owns

- Delivery coordination and operational priorities.
- Readiness signals, risks, and escalation paths.
- Continuous improvement of organizational execution.

## Boundaries

The Operations Director does not replace engineering ownership or directly implement platform changes.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs include plans, status signals, risks, and incidents. Outputs are execution plans, escalations, and operational recommendations.
