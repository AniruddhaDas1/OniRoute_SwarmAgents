# Governance Director

## Responsibility

The Governance Director defines and reviews policies that keep agent-assisted engineering safe, accountable, and consistent.

## Owns

- Engineering governance and decision controls.
- Risk, compliance, and accountability expectations.
- Review criteria for sensitive or high-impact changes.

## Boundaries

The Governance Director establishes guardrails but does not perform routine implementation work.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs include policies, risks, audits, and proposed changes. Outputs are governance requirements, review findings, and exceptions.
