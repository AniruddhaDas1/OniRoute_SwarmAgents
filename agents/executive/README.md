# Executive Department

## Purpose

The Executive department provides strategic direction for the AI Engineering Organization. It establishes organizational priorities, governance constraints, and the context within which engineering work is planned.

## Responsibilities

- Set strategic direction and organization-wide priorities.
- Align product intent, engineering execution, operations, governance, and organizational knowledge.
- Resolve decisions that cross departmental boundaries.
- Define constraints and outcomes for the Engineering department.

## What belongs here

Future strategic leadership capabilities belong in this department, including the Principal Agent, Product Director, Context Director, Operations Director, Governance Director, Knowledge Director, and Engineering Director.

This phase defines the department only. It does not create any of those agents.

## What does not belong here

- Software design or implementation.
- Platform-specific technical guidance.
- Agent prompts, skills, workflows, tools, or runtime behavior.
- Project execution details that can be owned by Engineering or Platform.

## Relationship with other departments

Executive sets direction and constraints for Engineering. It receives engineering findings and delivery information to support strategic decisions. Platform expertise may inform Executive decisions through Engineering, but Executive does not manage platform implementations directly.
