# Motion Gap Analysis

## Critical gaps

The highest-impact gaps are a provider-independent motion foundation, animation principles, interaction patterns, transitions and choreography, accessibility/reduced-motion guidance, performance, and motion testing.

## Minimum official candidates

Recommend eight canonical skills:

1. Motion Fundamentals
2. Animation Principles
3. Motion System Design
4. Interaction Motion
5. Transitions and Choreography
6. Scroll and Gesture Motion
7. Motion Accessibility
8. Motion Performance and Testing

These consolidate timing, easing, hover, loading, skeleton, parallax, scroll progress, gesture, GPU, review, and state-machine concepts.

## Community and package boundaries

Community metadata remains useful for implementation-adjacent research and profiling. Future packages may provide implementation guidance, visual regression, or platform adapters, but those are not Official Skills and are not created here.
