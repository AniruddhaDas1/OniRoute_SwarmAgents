# Admission Policy

Admission gates are evaluated in order:

1. Schema valid.
2. Metadata complete.
3. Provenance captured.
4. License accepted.
5. Dependencies valid or explicitly reviewable.
6. Compatibility evaluated.
7. Normalization successful and reversible.
8. Duplicate and version policy satisfied.
9. Registry admission approved.
10. Installation eligibility recorded separately.

Admission may produce Accepted, Rejected, Quarantined, Needs Review, Duplicate, or Superseded outcomes. Installation is never implied by admission. Policy exceptions require explicit authority, expiry, rationale, and retained evidence.
