# Ranking Model

Ranking is a transparent comparison of eligible candidates, not an authority to override policy gates. Suggested ordered signals are:

1. Exact capability match.
2. Exact Agent compatibility.
3. Exact sub-agent compatibility.
4. Context and tool contract fit.
5. Validation state and quality score.
6. Dependency health and lifecycle freshness.
7. License and provenance trust.
8. Current compatible version.
9. Provider and environment fit.
10. Popularity, only when future evidence is available and never as a substitute for validation.

The ranking record must expose signals, weights or precedence, ties, excluded candidates, and confidence. Equal scores require deterministic tie-breaking by explicit policy or a review result; silent arbitrary choice is not permitted.
