# Selection Policy

Candidates are eligible only when they satisfy the request and all mandatory policy gates. Selection considers:

- Official, Verified, Community, Enterprise, Personal, Imported, or Generated governance class.
- Validation state and quality score.
- Exact Agent and sub-agent compatibility.
- Semantic version and freshness requirements.
- License and organizational policy.
- Dependency health, completeness, and lifecycle state.
- Context and tool contract fit.
- Provider and environment constraints.
- Installation availability and deprecation status.

Selection must prefer explicit compatibility and evidence over popularity or name similarity. A lower-ranked candidate may be selected only with recorded rationale. Deprecated, disabled, archived, rejected, or unvalidated records are not eligible without an explicit policy exception.
