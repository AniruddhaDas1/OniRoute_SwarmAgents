# Skill Discovery

Discovery presents registry records by governance class and provenance:

- **Official Skills** — OniRoute-approved and maintained.
- **Community Skills** — externally contributed and validated to community gates.
- **Enterprise Skills** — organization-governed skills with private ownership or policy.
- **Personal Skills** — user-scoped skills with local ownership.
- **Imported Skills** — sourced from an external repository, archive, folder, or future registry.
- **Generated Skills** — produced by a future authorized generation process and subject to the same validation.

Classes are metadata views, not ownership transfers. A Skill may have one lifecycle state and multiple provenance or audience labels where policy permits. Discovery must expose license, source, version, validation state, quality score, dependencies, compatibility, and installation status before a consumer selects a Skill.
