# Skill Lifecycle

## States

1. **Draft** — metadata and contract are under authoring.
2. **Experimental** — available for controlled evaluation with explicit risk.
3. **Community** — reviewed for basic structure and provenance.
4. **Verified** — passed defined compatibility, contract, dependency, and quality checks.
5. **Official** — approved and maintained as an OniRoute-recommended Skill.
6. **Deprecated** — retained for migration with a replacement or retirement notice.
7. **Archived** — no longer approved for use but preserved for provenance.

## Transition Rules

Transitions require recorded validation evidence. A Skill may move forward only when its metadata, contract, dependencies, compatibility, and license status meet the target state. Deprecated Skills must not silently return to active states; they require a new review. Archived Skills are immutable historical records.
