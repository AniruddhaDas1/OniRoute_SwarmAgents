# Knowledge Source Trust Model

- **Official** — explicitly governed and maintained by OniRoute authority.
- **Verified** — identity, provenance, license, and discovery behavior passed defined checks.
- **Community** — externally maintained and cataloged with transparent provenance, but not OniRoute-governed.
- **Enterprise** — governed by an organization under documented access and policy controls.
- **Personal** — owned by an individual and limited by explicit consent and scope.
- **Experimental** — under evaluation with restricted use and declared risk.
- **Unknown** — insufficient evidence; not eligible for normal discovery or admission.

Trust is metadata, not a guarantee of asset correctness. Trust changes require recorded evidence, review authority, and an audit trail. A Source trust level does not automatically transfer to Skills or other assets discovered from it.
