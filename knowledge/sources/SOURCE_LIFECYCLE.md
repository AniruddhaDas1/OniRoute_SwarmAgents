# Knowledge Source Lifecycle

- **Discovered** — origin identified but not registered.
- **Registered** — canonical Source metadata recorded.
- **Indexed** — declared assets and source metadata indexed for discovery.
- **Validated** — identity, provenance, license, trust, and capabilities passed policy checks.
- **Available** — eligible for governed discovery.
- **Disabled** — temporarily unavailable by policy or operational decision.
- **Deprecated** — retained with a successor, migration, or retirement notice.
- **Archived** — historical source record retained and excluded from normal discovery.

Transitions require evidence and timestamps. Disabling or deprecating a Source must not erase provenance; archival preserves the record even when the origin is no longer reachable.
