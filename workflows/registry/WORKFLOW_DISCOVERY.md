# Workflow Discovery

Discovery is the metadata-based identification of registry records. Supported dimensions are Workflow ID, category, tags, Agent, sub-agent, Skill, Package, required or produced Artifact, platform, lifecycle, validation state, trust level, quality score, and owner.

Dimensions may be combined without implying rank, compatibility approval, dependency resolution, or execution. Discovery results must retain Workflow ID, version, classification, lifecycle, validation, trust, source, and provenance so consumers can evaluate suitability independently.
