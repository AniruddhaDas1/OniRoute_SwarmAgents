# Workflow Admission

A Workflow registry record is admitted only after evidence exists for:

1. **Metadata validation** — required fields are complete and conformant.
2. **Ownership validation** — owner and maintainers are identifiable and accountable.
3. **Compatibility validation** — declared Agents, Skills, Packages, and platforms are coherent.
4. **Dependency validation** — dependency identity, kind, version, optionality, and provenance are declared.
5. **Lifecycle validation** — lifecycle and status agree with the Workflow specification.
6. **License validation** — licensing is declared and compatible with registry policy.
7. **Provenance validation** — origin and history are complete and traceable.
8. **Quality review** — documentation, boundaries, reuse, and consistency are assessed.
9. **Architecture approval** — architectural scope and provider/runtime independence are approved.

Admission records the result, reviewer, date, evidence, exceptions, and applicable registry version. Rejection preserves rationale. Admission does not execute or endorse a Workflow beyond the recorded trust and validation levels.
