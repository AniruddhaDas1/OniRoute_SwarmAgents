# Workflow Provenance

Provenance is an immutable, append-only account of origin and registry governance. Every admitted record preserves:

- **Original Author** and **Original Organization**.
- **Original Source** and **Original Version**.
- **Registry Version** governing admission.
- **Admission Date** and admission evidence.
- **Review History**, including reviewer, date, outcome, and rationale.
- **Promotion History**, including lifecycle, trust, classification, or validation changes.

Corrections append superseding entries rather than rewriting historical facts. Derived, imported, or generated records must identify their parent source and transformation provenance. Provenance metadata does not retrieve, execute, or validate source content by itself.
