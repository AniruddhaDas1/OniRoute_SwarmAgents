# Workflow Indexing

Registry indexing projects admitted metadata into these logical facets:

- **Identity:** Workflow ID, display name, version, source, and ownership.
- **Category:** canonical category and classification.
- **Tags and Capabilities:** governed descriptors of declared outcomes and scope.
- **Participants:** compatible Agents and sub-agents.
- **Artifacts:** required and produced artifact declarations.
- **Dependencies:** Workflows, Skills, Packages, Knowledge Sources, and Artifacts.
- **Approvals:** declared approval kinds and authorities.
- **Decision Gates:** declared decision kinds and authorities.

Every index entry must retain a reference to its source registry record and version. Indexing does not inspect implementation, infer undeclared behavior, resolve dependencies, or define a storage or retrieval engine.
