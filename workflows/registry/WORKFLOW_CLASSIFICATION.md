# Workflow Classification

## Registry classifications

- **Official Workflows:** approved and maintained as OniRoute-recommended records.
- **Community Workflows:** community-owned records admitted under community governance.
- **Enterprise Workflows:** organization-governed records with enterprise ownership constraints.
- **Personal Workflows:** individually owned records with explicitly limited authority.
- **Experimental Workflows:** controlled evaluation records without production endorsement.
- **Imported Workflows:** records originating outside the registry with preserved provenance.
- **Generated Workflows:** externally generated definitions requiring explicit authorship, review, and provenance.

## Categories

Canonical categories are Architecture, Backend, Frontend, Database, Security, DevOps, AI, Testing, Documentation, Presentation, Analytics, Platform, Product, Operations, General Engineering, and Custom. A record has one primary category; tags may describe cross-cutting concerns. Classification describes governance origin or standing, while category describes engineering domain.
