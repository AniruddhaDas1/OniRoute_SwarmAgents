# Workflow Fallback

Fallback states cover **Missing Workflow**, **Missing Skill**, **Dependency Failure**, **Approval Failure**, **Compatibility Failure**, **Validation Failure**, **Superseded Workflow**, and **Deprecated Workflow**. A fallback declaration identifies trigger, eligible alternative or terminal state, authority, evidence, and policy constraints. Fallbacks may select another admitted contract or stop resolution; they must never bypass validation, licensing, security, ownership, or approval policy.
