# Workflow Conflicts

Conflict detection covers **Duplicate Workflow IDs**, **Conflicting Versions**, **Circular Workflow Dependencies**, **Conflicting Skills**, **Conflicting Packages**, **Conflicting Artifact Ownership**, and **Conflicting Approval Rules**. Resolution records the conflict, affected references, governing precedence or human decision, evidence, and outcome. Conflict handling is metadata-only; unresolved conflicts block a resolved Workflow.
