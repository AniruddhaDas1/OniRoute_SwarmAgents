# Workflow Context Flow

Context classes are **Workflow Input Context**, **Agent Context**, **Skill Context**, **Artifact Context**, **Decision Context**, **Approval Context**, and **Completion Context**. A composition declares what enters each boundary, what may be transformed or returned, what is retained or discarded, and the provenance of every retained value. Context flow follows minimization, authorization, security classification, and audit rules; no transport or storage mechanism is implied.
