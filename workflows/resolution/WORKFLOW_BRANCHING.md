# Workflow Branching

Branch declarations describe logical transitions only: **Linear**, **Conditional**, **Parallel (logical only)**, **Optional Step**, **Alternative Path**, **Review Gate**, **Human Approval**, **Rollback Path**, **Recovery Path**, and **Completion Path**. Each transition records its condition or gate, inputs, permitted destinations, authority, and audit requirement. Branches do not define scheduling, threads, retries, or other execution semantics.
