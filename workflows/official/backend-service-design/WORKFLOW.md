# Backend Service Design Contract

## Purpose

Define a repeatable collaboration contract for backend engineering.

## Scope

Covers analysis, collaboration, decisions, approvals, and metadata-only artifact declarations.

## Entry Criteria

An engineering outcome, constraints, accountable owner, and required context are declared.

## Exit Criteria

Outputs, artifacts, decisions, approvals, provenance, and review evidence are recorded.

## Participants

- Primary Agent: Backend Architect
- Primary Sub-Agent: Service Design Specialist
- Supporting Agents: Database, DevOps

## Required Skills

Service Design Specialist

## Produced Artifacts

- Architecture
- Source Code

## Decision Gates

Architecture Review

## Approval Gates

Architecture Review

## Inputs

Declared engineering outcome, constraints, and context.

## Outputs

Expected artifacts and recorded decisions.

## Dependencies

No additional dependencies are declared.

## Success Criteria

The contract is complete, internally consistent, reviewed, and traceable.

## Failure Conditions

Missing context, incompatible participants, unresolved conflicts, failed validation, or missing approval.

## Review Checklist

- Ownership is unique.
- Compatibility is declared.
- Artifacts and provenance are complete.
- No execution behavior is specified.

## Related Workflows

None.
