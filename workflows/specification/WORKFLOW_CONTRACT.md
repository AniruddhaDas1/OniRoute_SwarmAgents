# Workflow Contract

Every Workflow declares:

- **Purpose** and **Scope** — intended outcome and explicit boundaries.
- **Inputs** and **Outputs** — required information and expected results.
- **Artifacts** — metadata describing artifacts that may be produced.
- **Context** — entering, exchanged, retained, and returned context.
- **Participants** and **Responsibilities** — compatible Agents and their bounded duties.
- **Escalations** — conditions requiring a higher authority or human.
- **Decision Gates** and **Approval Gates** — explicit control points.
- **Failure Handling** — declared states and permitted recovery outcomes.
- **Completion Conditions** — observable contract-level success criteria.
- **Audit Requirements** — provenance, decisions, approvals, and validation evidence.

The contract describes obligations and boundaries, never implementation steps or execution mechanics.
