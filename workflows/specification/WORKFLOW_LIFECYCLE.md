# Workflow Lifecycle

1. **Draft** — being authored; not approved for use.
2. **Experimental** — controlled evaluation with explicit risk.
3. **Reviewed** — peer review completed and issues recorded.
4. **Verified** — metadata, compatibility, dependencies, and contract validated.
5. **Official** — approved OniRoute-recommended Workflow.
6. **Deprecated** — retained for migration with retirement or replacement guidance.
7. **Archived** — inactive historical record.
8. **Removed** — withdrawn from the catalog; references must explain disposition.

Transitions require evidence and ownership. Deprecated, Archived, and Removed Workflows must not silently return to active states.
